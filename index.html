About Me

Who I am

I'm Moosa Shahzain, an ambitious student currently in my first year of ICS at HITEC College in Wah Cantt / Taxila, Pakistan. I'm someone who genuinely enjoys the process of learning — whether that's writing my first lines of JavaScript, wiring up an Arduino circuit, or figuring out how a new AI tool works.

I like to experiment: taking things apart (figuratively and literally), testing ideas, and building small projects that teach me something new each time. My goal isn't to pretend I know everything — it's to keep levelling up, step by step, until I can create technology that's genuinely useful to people.
